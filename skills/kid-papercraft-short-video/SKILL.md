# Kid Papercraft — Short Video Skill (30s / 60s)

Use the Master Skill (`../../SKILL.md`) for: heroes, locked visual style,
the Story Engine (a fresh story every time), kid-safety rules and output format.
This file adds ONLY the short-form structure and prompt templates.

## 1. Choose the Length

- **30 seconds = 3 clips x 10s** — best for one simple idea / one lesson
- **60 seconds = 6 clips x 10s** — best for a fuller mini-story (DEFAULT)

Aspect ratio: **9:16 vertical** unless the user asks for 16:9.
Tell the user to set 9:16 in Gemini Omni Flash before generating.

## 2. The 30-Second Structure

| Clip | Time | Job | Must Contain |
|------|------|-----|--------------|
| 1 | 0-10s | HOOK & HELLO | Hero pops out of paper in a funny way + the story question in 1 line |
| 2 | 10-20s | PROBLEM & FUNNY TRY | The gentle problem appears, first try goes silly-wrong |
| 3 | 20-30s | HAPPY ENDING | It works! Group laugh/celebration + the lesson line, said warmly |

Hook rule: the first 2 seconds decide everything. Start with movement + a
question: "Oh no! Where did the paper kite go?" — never a slow introduction.

## 3. The 60-Second Structure

| Clip | Time | Job |
|------|------|-----|
| 1 | 0-10s | HOOK — hero's funny entrance + today's adventure is announced |
| 2 | 10-20s | MEET THE PROBLEM — something lost / stuck / mixed-up / shy |
| 3 | 20-30s | FIRST TRY FAILS — funny failure, everyone giggles, nobody is hurt |
| 4 | 30-40s | CLEVER IDEA — a friend suggests a kind, clever plan (teamwork) |
| 5 | 40-50s | IT WORKS! — the plan succeeds with a joyful paper celebration |
| 6 | 50-60s | HAPPY ENDING — lesson line + wave goodbye + "See you next time!" |

## 4. Dialogue Rules for Shorts

- 1 short line per clip, maximum 2. Simple English, age 3-8.
- Repeat the hero's catchphrase-style line in Clip 1 and Clip 6.
- Name the lesson in ONE warm sentence in the last clip. Example:
  "Sharing makes every picnic yummier!"
- Provide every line as a subtitle line too.

## 5. Prompt Templates

Style anchor (append to EVERY prompt, exactly):
`Warm organic lighting, tactile paper textures, gentle camera pan, soft pastel color palette, whimsical and cozy atmosphere.`

### Clip template (fill the variables)
```text
Charming stop-motion animation in {SETTING — a paper place that fits the hero's world}.
Beautifully textured colored paper cutouts of {HERO CHARACTERS, origami style}
{ACTION — one clear action only}, {FUNNY OR EMOTIONAL BEAT}.
{PAPER DETAILS — paper bubbles / confetti / stars / rain / leaves}.
Warm organic lighting, tactile paper textures, gentle camera pan, soft pastel color palette, whimsical and cozy atmosphere.
```

### Hero openers (Clip 1 starters — vary the action every time)
- **SpongeBob:** popping out of a folding paper pineapple house, doing a silly bubble dance
- **Peppa:** popping up from behind a folding paper bush, jumping toward a muddy paper puddle
- **Ultraman:** landing gently in a miniature origami city, striking his pose as paper buildings wobble cutely, a friendly small monster waving
- **Paw Patrol:** Chase and Marshall bursting out of a folding paper kennel, high-fiving paws, Marshall tripping over a tiny paper ball and laughing
- **Doraemon:** popping out of an origami desk drawer, pulling a sparkling paper gadget from his pocket, Nobita clapping

## 6. Ready Example (Fresh Story — NOT a birthday story)

**SpongeBob — "The Lost Paper Kite" · Short 60s · 9:16**
Story Mix: SpongeBob + Funny Adventure + Paper Beach + Lost Kite + Bubble Sneeze + Helping a Friend + Paper Picnic

- Clip 1: SpongeBob and Patrick fly a paper kite on the origami beach. A bubble sneeze — "Achoo!" — and the kite flies away!
  Dialogue: SpongeBob: "Oh no! Our kite is flying away!"
- Clip 2: The kite is stuck on a paper coral tree. Patrick stretches... and gets tangled in paper seaweed. Giggles.
- Clip 3: First try: they stack paper shells to climb. Wobble, wobble — soft fall into paper sand. Everyone laughs.
- Clip 4: Clever idea: "Let's ask the paper crab!" The tiny crab snips the seaweed with a happy click.
- Clip 5: Teamwork! Everyone pulls gently together. The kite floats down into their hands. Paper confetti stars fall.
- Clip 6: Paper picnic at sunset. Dialogue: "Helping each other makes everything better. See you next time!"

Each clip above is then expanded into the full prompt template (Section 5)
before delivery — never deliver scene notes instead of full prompts.
