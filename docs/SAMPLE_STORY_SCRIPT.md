## Pipo & Momo — "Pipo Learns to Fly" · Short 60s · 9:16 · Ages 4-6

**Kahini (one sentence):** Pipo, a tiny paper bird who has never flown, wants to fly
Momo's new kite with her because she made it just for him — but the wind steals the
kite into the high tree, and black paper rain clouds are coming that will melt it.
Pipo almost gives up ("I can't fly… I never could"), until he remembers Momo's words —
"Small wings fly too" — and flutters up, wobbly and brave, saving the kite seconds
before the rain. **Change: Pipo believes in himself.**

**Open question (suspense):** Will Pipo fly before the rain melts the kite?
**Emotion line:** Wonder → Giggle → Worry → Sad/Low → Hope → Brave → Proud → Warm
**Script score: 38/40** (Kahini 5/5 · Hook 5 · Clarity 5 · Funny 4 · Dialogue 5 · Lesson-shown 5 · Ending 5 · Talkability 4)

### Characters & Voices
- **Pipo** — tiny round blue origami bird, small orange beak. Voice: high, small, quick; repeats the last word when scared. Catchphrase: "Let's try!"
- **Momo** — small pink origami rabbit, long paper ears. Voice: soft, calm, sweet; hums when thinking. Catchphrase: "Small wings fly too."
