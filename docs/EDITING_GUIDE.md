# Pro Editing Guide — From 10-Second Clips to a Video Children Finish

Good editing is invisible: the story feels smooth, voices feel alive, jokes land.
Follow this order — never start with music or effects.

## 1. Assembly (Picture First)
1. Import clips in story order. Rename: `S01C01_hook`, `S01C02_problem`…
2. Trim the first and last **0.3s** of every generated clip (models wobble at the edges).
3. Cut on movement or on the blink — never mid-word.
4. Build the whole story with NO effects. Watch it silently: if the kahini is not
   clear with the sound off, fix the order/length now, not later.

## 2. Dialogue & Voice Pass
- Lay dialogue so it flows across cuts: **J-cut** = next speaker's voice starts
  ~0.2s before their picture (conversations feel natural); **L-cut** = keep the
  speaker's voice over the listener's reaction shot for emotional lines.
- One speaker at a time on the timeline too — no overlapping kid dialogue.
- Levels: dialogue peaks around -6dB; everything else lives underneath it.

## 3. Pacing
- Shorts: a visual change every **2-3 seconds** (cut, punch-in, or camera move).
  Punch-in zoom 100%→115% on the funniest moment; 0.4s freeze + boing on funny falls.
- Long: new place/character/song/funny beat every **60-90 seconds**; add chapter
  markers at every scene for the YouTube description.
- The Low Moment must BREATHE: hold it 1.5-2× longer than feels "efficient". Emotion needs air.

## 4. Transitions
- Between scenes: paper fold wipe / page turn / paper pop-up (the world is paper — transitions are too).
- Inside a scene: hard cuts only. No random spins, flashes or glitch effects — they break the cozy world.

## 5. Sound Design (after picture lock)
Paper world = paper sounds. Build a small library and reuse it all series:
- **Movement:** soft paper rustle · **Appearances:** pop · **Funny falls:** gentle boing
- **Magic/clues:** sparkle chime · **Success:** tiny paper-confetti shower + happy "yay" crowd giggle
- **Weather:** paper rain patter, soft wind whoosh (paper leaves)
Rules: SFX support the picture, never cover a word; the same event = the same sound, every episode
(kids learn the language of your show).

## 6. Music
- Style: cheerful ukulele / marimba / soft piano, no lyrics under dialogue.
- Beds by beat: Adventure (bouncy) · Worry (soft, slow, few notes) · Low Moment (almost
  silence — one soft piano line) · Climax (bed + sparkle) · Payoff (full happy theme).
- Duck music **-18dB under voices** (sidechain or manual keyframes). Swell 1s at the payoff.
- Final loudness ≈ **-14 LUFS** (YouTube/Shorts normalisation), true peak ≤ -1dB.

## 7. Subtitles (kids + parents read along)
- Every spoken line subtitled, word-for-word. Chunks of **2-4 words**.
- Font: big, rounded, bold, white with thick dark outline; the **keyword highlighted yellow**.
- Gentle bounce-in per chunk, timed to the first frame of speech.
- Position: Shorts → upper-middle safe zone (never under bottom UI or right-side buttons);
  Long → bottom-centre, above the lower third.
- Check every subtitle against the audio once, slowly. One wrong word = re-export.

## 8. Colour & Look (keep the paper world warm)
- Slight warmth + saturation (+5 to +10), soft contrast. Never cold/blue grades.
- Match clips: if one clip is duller, lift its brightness/saturation to its neighbours
  before adding any "look". Consistency > style.

## 9. Thumbnail & First Frame (part of editing, not an afterthought)
- Shorts: choose the most expressive frame (big face, mid-action) as the cover frame.
- Long: thumbnail = hero close-up + the story object + 2-4 word text, faces looking at camera.

## 10. Export Settings
| | Shorts/TikTok/Reels | YouTube Long |
|---|---|---|
| Size | 1080×1920 | 1920×1080 |
| FPS | 30 | 30 |
| Codec | H.264, 16-25 Mbps | H.264, 20-35 Mbps |
| Audio | AAC 320kbps, -14 LUFS | AAC 320kbps, -14 LUFS |
Also export from every Long episode: one vertical 60s cut-down (funniest scene) as a promo Short.

## 11. Final QC Checklist (watch the export once, fully, on a phone)
☐ Story clear with sound off ☐ Every word understandable with eyes closed
☐ No mid-word cuts ☐ Subtitles = audio, word-for-word ☐ Music never covers voices
☐ Low Moment breathes ☐ Payoff mirrors the opening ☐ Loudness ≈ -14 LUFS
☐ First 2 seconds contain motion + the hook ☐ Last frame loops (Shorts) or teases next episode (Long)
